Core Features

What ARYAN can do for you

🏥

Medical Q&A

Intelligent healthcare guidance powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash — ask anything from symptoms to treatment protocols.

🧭

Smart Navigation

ARYAN can navigate to any of the 10 HealthLens pages on your behalf using natural language commands.

🫁

Chest Disease Knowledge

Deep knowledge of TB, Pneumonia, COVID-19 detection. Guides users through X-ray analysis workflows.

🚨

Emergency Mode

Instantly switches to emergency guidance — provides first aid, calls to 108/112, and calm step-by-step instructions.

🎙️

Voice I/O

Full voice input via Web Speech API and optional Text-to-Speech output for hands-free operation.

💕

Emotional AI (Nikh Mode)

Activated by @Yadvi30 — a special emotionally intelligent mode with Hindi/English mix, songs, memories, and love meter.

📌

Message Management

Pin important messages, bookmark responses, react with emojis, copy to clipboard, and search through history.

⚡

Health Tools

Built-in BMI calculator, water tracker, step counter, mood check-in, calorie calculator, breathing exercises, and meditation timer.
